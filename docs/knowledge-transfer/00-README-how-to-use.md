# Knowledge transfer → SerMaStr — how to use these scaffolds

These four templates are containers for the *judgment layer* — the tacit, 30-years-earned reasoning a model can't infer. The SEO Fundamentals primer already captured the textbook (what things are, why tactics work). These capture how a master **decides, diagnoses, and anticipates**.

## How to fill them
- **Talk (or type) freely, out of order.** Every heading has prompting questions under it — answer whichever spark something. Messy, rambling, half-finished is fine. Speech-to-text is fine. Structuring it is my job.
- **Flag fact vs. theory.** The single most useful annotation: as you go, say whether a claim is *"this is how Google works"* (documented mechanics) or *"this is what I've seen / my operating theory"*. Just say "this is more of a theory" and I'll label it `(working model)` — SerMaStr is allowed to doubt those against a client's data, but not to override documented mechanics.
- **Concrete beats abstract.** Name real clients, real numbers, real situations wherever they come to mind. Examples are gold — they're what turn a rule into judgment.
- **Exceptions matter.** When a rule has a "usually X, but if Y then Z," say the Y and Z. The exception space is where expertise lives.
- **Contradict the primer / me freely.** If anything I wrote is wrong, oversimplified, or missing your nuance, say so. This should become *your* model.

## The four docs
1. **`01-strategists-playbook.md`** — prioritization & triage, risk & penalties, the limits of SEO, competitive strategy. *The decision-making layer.*
2. **`02-diagnostic-patterns.md`** — your symptom → cause → confirm → act library. *Pure pass-down; only you have this.*
3. **`03-seo-direction-of-travel.md`** — the history and where Google is heading, so SerMaStr can anticipate, not just react.
4. **`04-fundamentals-extensions.md`** — business-model→strategy translation, content-quality judgment, measurement wisdom. *Folds into the primer.*

## What I do when you hand these back
Route each piece to the right home (new SOP / primer section / module card / prompt), write it in the corpus's style, label fact vs. working-model, handle vendoring + signal-selection wiring + tests, and open a PR for your review. You never touch the plumbing.
