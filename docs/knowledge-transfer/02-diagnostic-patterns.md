# Diagnostic Patterns — your symptom → cause library
*(This one is pure pass-down — only you have it. Dictate as many patterns as you can. Don't polish; a one-line pattern is worth more than a perfect paragraph.)*

Destined for a new `docs/sops/Diagnostic_Patterns.md`. The goal: give SerMaStr your *pattern recognition* — the "I've seen this shape before" that lets a veteran diagnose from a constellation of symptoms instead of running a checklist.

## How to add a pattern
For each one, just talk through as many of these as you can (skip any that don't apply):
- **Symptom(s)** — what you observe (the shape: what moved, what didn't, how fast, where).
- **Likely cause(s)** — what it usually is, most-likely first.
- **How to confirm** — the check that proves or rules it out.
- **What to do** — the fix, and the order.
- **How long to see movement** — realistic timeline.
- **Exceptions / look-alikes** — what else produces the same shape, and how to tell them apart.
- **Confidence** — is this a strong tell or a weak hint?

You don't need the table format — just say them. Below are starter slots across the common shapes to jog memory; add your own freely.

- Everything **improved then reverted** a week later →
- Ranks great **near the office, dies past a few miles** (local) →
