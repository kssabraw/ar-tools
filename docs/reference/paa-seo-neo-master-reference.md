# PAA → SEO Neo — Master System Reference

> **What this is.** A single consolidating reference for a local-SEO methodology: how the **PAA content layer** and the **SEO Neo authority layer** fit into one machine, where they connect, in what order, and with what confidence. It is a *synthesis* of the source corpus (the two "why" keystone docs, the integrated playbook, and SOPs 01–15 + 07b/13b), not a replacement for them — each section points to the source doc that owns the detail.
>
> **Confidence legend.** **[PROVEN]** = a case study / repeated result in the transcripts · **[THEORY]** = a stated model, unproven or disputed · **[BELIEF/EVOLVING]** = a working assumption that shifts with Google. Treat THEORY/BELIEF as heuristics, not laws.
>
> **Provenance & caveat.** Synthesized from a private local-SEO mastermind's training transcripts — **not** official Google guidance. Some tactics are gray/black-hat and carry ToS/business risk; note also a disclosed commercial incentive in the source material — its authors have a financial stake in the primary link-building tool it recommends — so weigh its enthusiasm accordingly. Prefer the white-hat path where a choice exists. See §10.

## 1. The one-sentence thesis

**Make one business topically undeniable for one service in one place — across every content format a machine can read — then make it *trusted* with links and real engagement, and repeat per service.**

Everything else in this document is a consequence of that sentence.

## 2. The two-layer model (the spine)

The system is **one machine with two layers that do different jobs on the same target.**

| | **Layer 1 — Relevancy / content** | **Layer 2 — Authority / links** |
|---|---|---|
| **Job** | Make the page *deserve* to rank (who you are / what you do / where) | Make the relevant page *trusted* enough to actually rank |
| **Engine** | The PAA method | SEO Neo + off-Neo tier ones + PRs |
| **SOPs** | 05, 06, 07, 07b, 07c, 08, 09, 10, 13b | 01, 02, 03, 04, 04b, 14, 15 |

**The mutual dependency (why neither is skippable):**
- **Content alone ranks only in easy markets.** [PROVEN] A free Pictory + inVideo + GBP post + blog moved a grid **16→12 in a week** with no links; national PAAs have ranked with zero link building.
- **Content alone will NOT rank in competitive niches.** [PROVEN] "PAAs alone won't rank — need authority/trust/engagement" (car-accident/plastic-surgery/roofing in big geos).
- **Authority pointed at thin/off-topic content is wasted.** [PROVEN by counter-example] Links carry entity signals; land them on mixed-service content and Google discounts them. A GBP mixing handyman/appliance/HVAC "ranked terribly" despite 1,000 reviews.

**Consequence:** do Layer 1 *well* or Layer 2 has nothing worth amplifying — which is why the whole system is really about the **seam** between them (§5).

## 3. The atomic unit and the hand-off artifact

**The PAA string is the universal join key.** One exact-match "People Also Ask" question is filed *identically* as: the blog H2/title, image alt text, video title, podcast title, GBP post, **Neo content bucket**, **RD-100 anchor text**, and **wiki/cloud embed**. "Exact-match everywhere" is the entity-resolution mechanism ("no confusion" about who/what/where), not a style choice. Get the PAA right once → it propagates; wrong → the error propagates just as far.

**The prep sheet is the physical hand-off.** One per client: NAP, CID, place ID, GBP URLs, every blog/social/podcast/video URL, image URLs, embeds. The integrated playbook states that this sheet *is* the seam — "most tactics are just this sheet routed to a different destination." The one real organizational gap the transcripts flag: **agree explicitly who captures the asset URLs and hands them to the Neo operator** (RD 100 and the content bucket both depend on that list existing).

## 4. The first principles (the operating "why")

The recurring mental models that drive every rule (from the whole-system why-doc §3):

1. **Google ranks pages, not websites** [BELIEF] → one page per PAA/service; cram audio+video+image+schema onto that one page; link to the *service page*, not the homepage.
2. **Identity signal = who you are / what you do / where you do it** → stated identically everywhere, at the **service level**.
3. **Exact-match everywhere** [BELIEF] → consistency beats variety (variety is deliberately introduced only in *tier-one* anchor text).
4. **Topical congruence / entity discipline** [PROVEN] → every asset in a campaign is about the **same one service**; mixing services is the #1 failure mode (the single most-repeated discipline in the source material).
5. **The service keyword is the unit of work** → "metal roof repair," not "roofing" (revenue + buyer intent + easier to rank + clean entity).
6. **Multimedia = multiple algorithm surfaces** → "audio, video, images, content — tie it together with schema, engagement on top." Each format occupies a surface the others can't.
7. **Two ways to show Google: links + real traffic** → pair link building with referral-journey engineering (Twitter→YouTube→SoundCloud→blog).
8. **Branded = safe** [BELIEF] → a branded anchor can't over-optimize you; ≥50% branded; risky high-volume campaigns use branded/plain URLs.
9. **Tier ones as "pillows"** [THEORY-leaning] → aggressive/exact-match links point at tier ones that link the service page — a deletable buffer + over-optimization insulation for the money site.
10. **Real beats AI** [BELIEF] → real footage/humans/location anchors send signals AI can't fake (the basis of SOP 13b).
11. **Single-variable testing** [PROVEN methodology] → one action, then scan, so cause is attributable — on **owned/donor assets, never clients**.
12. **Adapt to the SERP** → the nav-block order (All/Maps/Images/Short Videos/Forums/…) is Google telling you which format wins that query; read it, don't hardcode.

## 5. The seam — how the two layers physically connect

### 5.1 The seven "bolts" (the mechanical hand-off)
1. **PAA articles feed Neo content buckets — per service, even per PAA**, with *that PAA's* Canva image + video inside the bucket.
2. **RD 100 runs to** the PAA blog posts, the PAA social posts, and the **tier ones** that link the service page.
3. **The GMBB Blast powers the one service the PAA targets** (via *directional URLs* — Google's own map assets, so safe to hammer; keep each blast's keywords to one service).
4. **Wiki/cloud stacks embed the exact PAA article** (and its podcast/video) — stacking authority *onto* the asset, not just near it.
5. **PAAs feed Rank Your Brand tier-3** (LSI/entity anchors).
6. **The full PAA (any length) is used as RD-100 anchor text.**
7. **The batch loop:** grab all a topic's PAAs → write Neo articles → hit all tier ones → RD 100 to all tier ones.

### 5.2 The fixed order (and why the waits are load-bearing)
**Content FIRST → GMBB Blast on that service → wait ~2 weeks (settle) → RD 100 to the PAA assets/tier ones → re-scan → rinse per service.**

- **Content first** — "the posts are key; you have to do posts first before you stack up." Authority has nothing to amplify until the content exists.
- **Never RD 100 + a Blast on the *same money URL* at once** — two simultaneous authority signals at one URL confuse Google. (A Blast to directional URLs + RD 100 to tier ones is fine — *different targets*.)
- **The settle waits are safety + attribution**, not politeness: over-blasting tanks rankings, and spacing preserves single-variable legibility. Model the waits as first-class steps — never fire the layers in parallel.
- **Re-scan timing differs by tactic:** ~3–4 days after a Blast; **weeks** after RD 100 (link-based moves "take a month to index").
- **Rinse every 6 weeks – 3 months** — ranking slips as Google shrinks map proximity; this is a maintenance loop, not a one-shot.

### 5.3 The gate (single-variable decision)
Do the content layer, let it sit ~1 week, scan, then branch: **moved → next topically-related service; no movement → drill deeper (more sub-PAAs, ≤~4 levels) or power with Neo; main revenue service still stuck after drilling → HALT and re-check on-page/entity** (something upstream is wrong; more PAAs won't fix it).

## 6. The authority supply chain (where Layer 2's power comes from)

Layer 2 is itself a two-stage pipeline: **build/acquire tier ones → RD-100 them → tier ones link the service page (never the homepage) → the service page ranks.** Tier ones come from three converging sources:

- **SEO Neo** cloud/wiki stacks that embed the PAA article — SOPs 01–04, 04b (the decision layer: which wizard when, LOS vs SAB, CID 50–80 first-run then place ID, anchor text, SERP audit).
- **Off-Neo (SOP 14)** — Neo deliberately does **not** build PBNs. Separate discipline: PBNs, expired/auction domains, Turbo subdomains, parasites/EMDs, citations. Also owns "what a tier one actually is" + the safety rules.
- **Press releases (SOP 15)** — a **maps play, not organic**: hundreds of referring domains + News-tab coverage, used as tier ones / RD-100 targets.

The **GMBB Blast** is a *parallel, map-only* amplifier (directional URLs) that never touches the money URL — which is why it can run alongside RD 100.

**Wizard purposes (SOP 04/04b), one-liners:** DAS = warm a cold profile with plain URLs (can't over-optimize) · Sniper = branded authority to site+GBP (CID first-run only, 50–80) · GMBB Blast = mass blog-comments to directional URLs (LOS only — needs an address) · Rank Your Brand = own the brand SERP (branded→primary→entity) · Cloud/Wiki = embed the asset, fast-indexed tier ones · RD 100 = ≥100 referring domains to each tier one.

## 7. The connective + support layer

- **Schema (SOP 09)** — the literal "tie it together": JSON (Audio/Video/FAQ) handed to the crawler/LLM so the multimedia is machine-readable and snippet/AI-overview-eligible.
- **Images (SOP 10)** — watermarked so Google Vision OCR reads NAP/keyword/logo; hosted on the client's site; **reused as the Neo bucket images** (a seam).
- **Snippets (SOP 08)** — one rich snippet wins a whole keyword cluster; snippet *tapering* is the trigger to fire RD 100.
- **Podcast/audio (07b)** — a *bridge asset* spanning both layers: produced from the PAA blog at near-zero marginal cost (rips the video's audio), it says Layer-1 content *and* produces Layer-2 authority (one upload → ~13 syndicated brand links + a knowledge panel). Authority play, not audience play (~5–7 real downloads).
- **Real footage + influencer (13b)** — the "real human" seam: field footage → GBP back end (a signal AI can't fake); the influencer's **permanent embedded endorsement + backlink** is the SEO deliverable; feeds video/PR pipelines; more lead-gen/referral than ranking.
- **Video (07c) — MISSING** — the production hub that supplies 07b's audio and 13b's reels; the one structural gap in this corpus.

## 8. The measurement + agency operating model

The SEO is downstream of a business model (whole-system why-doc §6):
- **Chase revenue keywords, not vanity keywords** — call tracking tags which *service* drove each call; optimize to the phone ringing.
- **Get services from the client, drill to the highest-revenue underperforming service** (don't guess from the website).
- **Price to cost-to-rank** (competitiveness = review counts + exact-match competitors), not a flat rate.
- **Rev-share / broker / DBA models** align payment with results.
- **Over-report + staff fulfillment** — churn is relational; weekly touches reduce it.
- **Single-variable testing on owned assets, not clients** — protect client results while still learning.

## 9. Confidence map (what to trust vs test vs verify)

- **[PROVEN] — act on directly:** the two-layer dependency; topical congruence; PAA→service-page linking; GMBB Blast moving donor maps (Denver plumber; commercial-roofing isolation test); wikis as the strongest link group; podcasts generating/preserving a knowledge panel; Pictory videos outranking DIY; single-variable methodology.
- **[THEORY — configurable, test first:]** the RD-100 "100 inbound per 1 outbound" number (one contributor's model; **openly disputed by others in the source material — a common alternative default is ~50 referring domains**); exact tier counts; tier-one "pillow" mechanics; DAS as required warm-up.
- **[BELIEF/EVOLVING — verify against the current SERP:]** exact-match-everywhere; the organic-vs-maps URL decision; the LLM/GEO source ladder; map-proximity shrinkage; experimental schema/VideoObject tests.
- **Contradictions to preserve, not resolve** (surface both, default conservative): exact-match on the money site (one camp keeps it brand-only vs another links exact-PAA directly); blast cadence ("never weekly" vs "weekly is fine"); naked vs geo PAA.

## 10. Pitfalls, risk & ethics posture

**Seam pitfalls (encode as validations, not style notes):**
- Over-blasting a service → fluctuation/tank (conservative cadence is a safety mechanism).
- RD 100 + Blast to the *same money URL* at once → confusion (space ~2 weeks; different targets are fine).
- Cannibalization → never reuse an identical PAA slug across cities; **~1,000 neighborhood pages inside one city is the named failure** (geo-in-URL city pages are fine).
- Entity/service pollution → one service per bucket / per tier one; never mix.
- Spinning money-site content → don't (spin only lower tiers / RD-100 volume).
- Velocity → 1–3/day, never a Monday, no bursts.
- Hard prerequisite → the service page must already be optimized, or the PAA work is wasted.

**Ethics / ToS:** CTR/engagement manipulation is **currently PAUSED** ("does more harm than good now" — reversed from 4 years ago). A meaningful slice of the method is gray/black-hat (mass blog-comment blasting, PBNs, spun tier content, directional-URL blasting, footprint management, parasites/EMDs). Document accurately; do not present as Google-endorsed; prefer the white-hat path (real content, real citations, PRs, genuine engagement) where a choice exists.
