01

Before you start

The page always renders — no hard gate. Every section degrades independently: a missing or failing data source is simply omitted from the PDF, never a blocker for the rest of the report.

Email needs SMTP configured agency-wide; a Drive copy needs a Drive folder set on the client. Neither failing blocks the report — delivery is recorded "skipped," not "failed."

02

Generate on demand

Report type — Monthly SEO or AI Visibility (a third, Local Rank/Maps, is schedule-only — see below). Period — 30/60/90/120 days, last year, or since campaign start. Tick "Email & save to Drive when done" to deliver this one report; a scheduled run always delivers regardless. Click Generate report — the history table polls while anything's pending. Download always re-signs the link fresh.

03

The standing schedule

Recipients (comma-separated), Schedule — Off / Weekly / Monthly with a day and hour — and "Report covers", which defaults to Auto (7 days weekly, 30 monthly) or a fixed period if you want it to look further back than the delivery cadence.

two extras ride the same clock

"AI Visibility report" and "Local Rank (Maps) report" — each only actually generates for a client tracking the matching keywords, so it's safe to leave both on for every client.

04

Read the history

Columns: Generated · Type · Period · Includes (which sections actually came back with data) · Status · Delivered (separate ✓/✗/— marks for email and Drive, hover a ✗ for the stored error) · PDF.

05

Troubleshooting

A section is blank in the PDF? Not a bug — that section had no data or failed to gather, and was simply left out rather than blocking the report.

Delivered shows a dash, not a checkSMTP isn't configured, or Recipients is empty — both cause a silent "skipped."
A toggled extra report never showed upThe client doesn't track the matching keywords yet — deliberate, not a bug.
Old download link 404sShouldn't happen — Download always re-fetches a fresh signed URL first.
