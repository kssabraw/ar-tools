01

Before you start

A connection bar at the top tells you the state: green means connected, blue means someone staff+ needs to click Connect, grey means an admin hasn't configured it. A verified location is required to publish — drafting works either way.

Freeze pauses publishing, not drafting — a frozen client blocks Publish now and scheduled auto-publish, but drafting and syncing keep working.

02

Compose

Pick a type — Update, Offer, Event, Product — each with its own fields (Offer/Event add title + dates, Offer adds a coupon code). Draft with AI generates copy grounded in the client's brand voice + ICP. "Create posts from a page URL" drafts several distinct posts (1–99) from one page, each with a different angle so they don't read like paraphrases. Image field: Upload, Generate with AI, From URL, or Reuse existing (the client's own prior blog/Local SEO images).

03

Posts

Filter chips — All / Drafts / Scheduled / Live — with live counts. Sync from Google pulls in anything posted directly there. Status badges: Draft, Scheduled (with the time), Publishing…, Live (with a View on Google link), Rejected/Failed, Deleted.

04

Publish

Publish now goes live immediately. A future datetime pick or the recurring schedule with auto-publish on both go out on their own — the status badge is always the tell for which kind a post is.

05

Recurring Schedule

Off / Weekly / Every 2 weeks / Monthly, an hour, a post type, and theme/rotation notes to guide the AI draft.

off by default, on purpose

Auto-publish is a separate checkbox — scheduled posts land as drafts for review unless you turn it on, which shows an explicit warning: posts will publish live with no human review.

06

Trash

Deleted posts get Restore or permanent delete, plus Empty trash — though a post still live on Google is kept out of a bulk empty until it's removed from Google first.

07

Troubleshooting

Scheduled auto-publish didn't go outCheck if the client is frozen — due posts skip or hold until it lifts.
AI copy ignores brand voiceIt only pulls from the client's saved Voice Card / ICP — fill those in first.
Image got rejectedJPG/PNG only, ≥250×250px, 10KB–25MB.
