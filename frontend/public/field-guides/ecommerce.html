01

Before you start

Brand Voice and ICP are shared with the rest of the suite — set once, used everywhere. A house template (Step 2) makes every new product page mirror a reference layout you already like, adapting the copy per product rather than reusing generic boilerplate. Collections don't use a house template.

read before you ship

Public specs get auto-researched, but always click through and confirm the cited source actually says what the page claims — the panel is flagged "verify" for a reason.

A frozen client can't generate or reoptimize.

02

Write a new page

Open the client → Ecommerce Writer → New Page. Pick Product or Collection at the top — it changes the form and the writing rubric underneath.

  • Target keyword — e.g. "wireless noise-cancelling headphones."
  • Source URL (optional) — scrapes a live page for real facts.
  • Product details (optional) — paste specs/price/variants directly.
  • Notes (optional) — freeform guidance for the writer.

Product vs. Collection. Product is the workhorse — one page for one item, and most sessions will be this. Collection is for a category/listing page that groups several products together — use it only when the client's site actually has a category landing page structure worth covering, not for every product.

Source URL vs. Product details. Give a Source URL when a real page already exists somewhere (the client's own site, a manufacturer's page, a competitor's listing) — it scrapes real facts instead of generic ones. Paste into Product details when you have the specs/price/variants in hand but no single URL to point at — a spec sheet, an internal doc, a conversation with the client. Do both when you have a starting URL but want to add or correct specifics it's missing.

House template (products only) — paste a reference product page's URL and click Save to set the client's default; the writer reproduces that page's section structure for every product page after.

When a house template is worth setting. Worth it once you've generated a page or two you're happy with and want every future product page to match that layout automatically — most useful on a client with many similar products (variants of one line, a consistent catalog structure). Skip it for a one-off page or a client whose products don't share a common shape.

Click Create product/collection page — 10–12 minutes, or Leave & finish in the background. "Bulk from a keyword list" switches to a mode where each line becomes its own background job with a shared batch-wide notes field (keyword-only — no per-item facts in bulk mode yet).

Single page vs. bulk. Use single-page generation whenever you have real facts to hand it (a Source URL or pasted details) — bulk mode can't take per-item facts yet, so a bulk-generated page leans more on the model's own research. Bulk is the better call for a long keyword list where individually sourcing facts for each one isn't worth the time, or for a first pass you plan to review and reoptimize with real facts afterward.

03

Review the auto-sourced facts

A green "Auto-sourced public specs — verify" panel lists each researched fact with its value, unit, and a source link — identity/handling specs (CAS, formula, weight, solubility, reconstitution) and receptor-pharmacology facts only. Clinical efficacy, dosing, and regulatory claims are hard-excluded, never researched.

A "How to reach 100/100" card, a Search coverage panel, and the brand-voice panel all sit alongside it — plus Preview / HTML / JSON-LD Schema tabs and a featured-image picker at the bottom of the page.

04

Score & reoptimize

Score tab — composite score and per-engine breakdown, no rewriting. A freshly generated page also gets a one-click "Score & Improve" shortcut straight into this view.

Reoptimize tab — a "Page URLs — one per line" box (one line handles a single page fine); each line can carry URL | keyword. Or switch the mode pills to "Discover from site" and click Discover product pages / Discover collection pages to crawl the client's sitemap for candidates.

Score first, or straight to Reoptimize? Score first when you're not sure a page actually needs work — it's the read-only, no-cost check. Go straight to Reoptimize when you already know it's underperforming (a client complaint, a rank drop) and just want it fixed.

Paste URLs vs. Discover from site. Paste when you already know exactly which pages need attention. Use Discover when you don't have a working list of the client's product/collection URLs — it crawls the sitemap for you, which is slower but saves you from hunting them down by hand.

A page gets rewritten when it's below 75/100 on SEO, or fails its brand-voice bar — clearing both is a deliberate skip. Add your own notes to force a rewrite regardless of score. Up to 3 rewrite-and-rescore passes run under the hood, keeping the best result.

When to force a rewrite with notes. A page can clear both bars and still be worth touching — new specs to add, a promotion to mention, a competitor angle you want addressed. The score gate protects against needless spend; your own notes are the deliberate override when you know something the scorer doesn't.

05

Saved Pages & Drafts

Same soft-delete pattern as Local SEO: delete from Saved Pages → lands in Drafts → Restore or delete permanently, or Empty drafts clears it all.

06

Publishing

Publish to Google Doc and Publish to WordPress live on the detail view. Re-publishing creates a new Doc/post each time — it does not update the previous one, so avoid clicking Publish repeatedly on a page already live.

Which destination. Google Doc is the review copy — use it when the client wants to sign off before anything goes live. WordPress publishes straight to the client's actual site — reserve it for a page you're confident is ready, since (per the note above) hitting Publish again doesn't update the live one, it creates a second post.

Blocked by a voice violation? Fix the wording, or Publish anyway to override deliberately — same rule as everywhere else in the suite.

09

Practice exercises

  • Generate a product page from a Source URL and read the auto-sourced specs panel.
  • Set a house template, then generate a second product and compare the structure.
  • Score a page, then Reoptimize it with a note and check the before/after.
  • Publish to Google Doc, then publish again and confirm it created a new Doc.
