01

Before you start

This is not the mass-content generator — it doesn't write anything. It answers one question, "what should we target, and why," and hands you a filtered keyword universe plus a ranked list of topic ideas.

Every run spends a small daily budget of paid calls, shared agency-wide — the page shows "Budget left today: N calls" at the top, red at zero.

only as good as the ground

The tool researches around what you type. If the client's website, GBP category, and location aren't filled in, "Suggest topics" and "Topic Research" have far less to work with.

02

Research keywords

Type into the seed box — one per line, or comma-separated. Multiple seeds are fully supported and researched together (a run always keeps at least 2 once you've added a second, capped at 20). No idea where to start? Click Suggest topics — it reads the client's GBP category, location, website, and ICP and proposes seeds as click-to-add chips.

Click Research keywords — a background job, so you don't have to sit and wait.

03

Read the results

A cluster rail up top — one chip per auto-grouped topic — filters the table below; a Questions only checkbox narrows to question-form keywords. Columns: Keyword, Cluster, Volume, CPC, Comp, KD, Intent, Relevance (% match to this client's topic/audience), and Opportunity (value × ease × intent — the closest thing to "worth the most"). Run-history chips let you flip between past runs without re-researching.

04

What got filtered, and why

The pool runs through several relevance, audience, and drift filters before you ever see it — and it shows its work. A "What we filtered & why" panel appears with a one-line summary ("N candidates found · N kept · N filtered out") and reason chips: unrelated brand or namespace, only a generic seed word, off-topic category drift, wrong audience (job-seeker), or a navigational/competitor lookup. Click "Show the N filtered-out keywords" to see the dropped terms next to their reason.

If a lot of what got cut looks genuinely on-topic to you, that's a real signal — broaden your seeds or add another one.

05

A thin run

Fewer than ~20 keywords triggers a plain-language callout: nothing came back at all (too narrow — try dropping a qualifier), plenty came back but almost everything got filtered (points at the panel above), or just a generic thin result (broaden or add a second seed). Below it, broader topic suggestions auto-fetch as click-to-add chips, and a "Research topics instead" button jumps straight into Topic Research using this run's seeds.

06

Manage your seeds

Remove a seed with the × on its chip (only when a run has >2 seeds — deletes only the keywords that seed alone produced). Every People-Also-Ask question has a "+ seed" link, plus a bulk "Add all as seeds" above the list. Clear all for a client is permanent — runs, keywords, and reports all go.

07

Topic Research (BETA)

A deeper pass — instead of expanding your seed's wording, it starts from the client's real buyer problems (ICP + site themes), validates each idea against real search demand (PAA + suggestion volume), and mines what competitors publish on the same themes. Click Research topics (~30 seconds, spends more budget than a plain run). Read the Strategy callout first, then browse topic cards grouped under pillars — buyer problem, intent/funnel, supporting keywords, real reader questions, a priority badge. A card marked covered means the client likely already has content on it — tick Show gaps only to hide those.

08

Write this post

Every topic card has a Write this post button — a modal pre-fills the seed keyword and a full angle brief (working title, buyer problem, intent/funnel, reader questions, related keywords), editable before you submit. Create draft queues a real Blog Writer run, pre-loaded with your angle as writer notes.

For a batch instead of one at a time: select rows in the table and use Send to Content Scheduler — this queues into AR Tools' own native bulk page-creation tool, a different tool from Topic Fanout (see the Troubleshooting table).
