## Why the source matters

A single study rarely settles anything. What moves the needle is the weight of
evidence: several independent groups, different methods, converging on the same
answer.

- **Randomised trials** are strongest for "does this cause that".
- **Cohort studies** are useful when a trial would be impractical.
- **Case reports** describe what happened once, and are a starting point, not a conclusion.

## Reading a claim

When you see a headline, three questions get you most of the way:

1. Who was studied, and are they like you?
2. How big was the effect, in numbers you can picture?
3. Compared with what?
