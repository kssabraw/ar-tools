## Why a guide like this exists

Most claims you meet in the wild are neither wholly true nor wholly false. The
skill worth building is not memorising answers but knowing how to weigh an answer
you have never seen before. Each section below covers one part of that skill and
links to a deeper post.

## Where the evidence comes from

Not all sources carry the same weight. A guide to reading them starts with the
hierarchy of evidence and why it exists.

## Reading a single claim

Once you can place a source, the next skill is reading one specific claim without
being misled by how it is framed.

## Putting it together

The parts reinforce each other. Read widely, weigh the source, and check the
claim against what else you know.
